Scrambled Eggs

Description

A quick and efficient recipe for scrambled eggs. Nothing too flashy, but the perfect breakfast to start your day with!

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Salt (Preferred)
  • Pepper (Preferred)
  • Olive or sunflower oil (Olive preferred)

Steps

  1. Crack your desired amount of eggs into a bowl
  2. Pour proper amount of milk
  3. Whisk eggs with milk until there is no more yolk bits left
  4. Turn on stove and preheat pan greased with oil
  5. Pour the whisked mixture immediately
  6. Let it cook and monitor the mixture
  7. Once the pan heats up enough and mixture starts to bubble, keep mixing it to avoid burned bits
  8. Once at least 5 minutes of proper cooking has passed, pour salt and pepper and mix it for another minute, then turn the stove off
  9. Pour scrambled eggs into plate, add your spices or vegetables of choice and enjoy!
