Lasagna Recipe

Description

In this wonderful recipe, sheets of pasta are covered with cheesy filling, and a rich meaty tomato sauce, and more cheese and then baked until bubbly and browned.

A recipe for the family or friends or your significnant other. Beloved by many, tasty for ages!

Ingredients

  • Cheese
  • Pasta
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Dills (optional)

Steps

  1. Prepare Ingredients
  2. Set water to boil
  3. Grate cheese
  4. Once water is boiling, set pasta to boil
  5. Prepare a sheet
  6. Once pasta is ready, pour the first sheet
  7. Then pour cheese and tomato sauce ontop
  8. Keep doing this for as many layers as you can or want!
  9. Set in preheated oven for 35 minutes
  10. Once done, set to table and serve!
