Pizza Recipe

Description

An italian classic, a meal that is a wonderful size for two or more people! Delicious, creamy and cheesy, like a proper pizza!

Ingredients

  • Dough (Sorry, not making dough in this recipe!)
  • Cheese
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Basil leaves
  • Pepperoni (or Salami, depending on preference!)

Steps

  1. Prepare dough on table or a plate
  2. Pour and spread the tomato sauce across the dough evenly
  3. Grate cheese (If you have grated cheese, skip this.)
  4. Pour grated cheese onto the sauce covered dough base
  5. Put pepperonis ontop of the cheese and sauce covered dough base
  6. Pre-heat oven for 3 minutes on 200 degrees Celsius
  7. Put the dough into the oven for 25 minutes on 200 degrees Celsius
  8. Pull out pizza and place on plate, and serve!
